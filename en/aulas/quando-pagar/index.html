When to pay: DataForSEO, Firecrawl, Apify, Semrush, and Ahrefs

The five tools cover your own site. Market and competitors are a different thing: someone collected the data and charges for it. Paying makes sense when a decision depends on data that only exists as a paid product, for a set period and with a spending cap. This lesson gives you the criterion, shows what each service delivers, and closes with your own tool plan.

02 · What each one delivers

Prices change fast: always check the vendor's site. Tap each one.

1. DataForSEO + open-seo

open-seo is an open (MIT) alternative to Semrush and Ahrefs, with an MCP server and skills for agents. But the data comes from DataForSEO, paid per call: the README says you need the key either way, and the hosted service charges 28% extra on every request.

It's an interface over paid data, not a data source of its own.

2. Firecrawl

Scrape, crawl, map, and search: turns pages into Markdown and JSON for content audits and briefs. Pricing page read on 10/19/2026: free plan with 1,000 credits per month, 1 credit per page; paid plans start at US$ 16 per month with annual billing. Check the site.

Its search is not Google's SERP.

3. Apify

A store of bots (actors) that scrape SERPs with related questions, Google Maps, and more. Pricing page read on 10/19/2026: free with US$ 5 in credit; Starter at US$ 19 per month. Each actor has its own price.

Whoever runs an actor that scrapes Google takes on the terms violation; quality and price vary by author.

4. Semrush, Ahrefs

Volume, difficulty, competitors, backlinks, and AI monitors, with an official MCP. AI visibility monitors run from about US$ 20–100 per month with a few questions to US$ 300–1,500+ with volume, according to third parties (Jul–Oct/2026).

Share of voice is a sample of the tool's question list, not an official score.

2026 yardstickExact search volume doesn't exist for free. A paid AI monitor is a sample; the official, free one is Bing AI Performance. A manual battery of 20–30 questions per month is still worth it.
