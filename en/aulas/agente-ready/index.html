Lesson 3 · 16 min

agente-ready: a 0–100 score with weights you can change

"AI readiness" scanners give you a score, but almost none publish how it is calculated. agente-ready keeps each weight, the reason, and the source in a versioned file, pesos.json. You get your site's score, see what weighs most, and if you disagree with a weight, you change it and run again.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 6 categories and the weight of each criterion
  2. 2How the score is calculated (and what leaves the denominator)
  3. 3Why llms.txt weighs 2 and WebMCP is preparation
  4. 4Run with your own weights

Goal: get your site's readiness score and change one weight in pesos.json to see the score change.

Weights add up to 100. Tap each step.

02 · The criteria that weigh the most

Tap each group.

1. Access

robots.txt lets search bots (12) and user-requested agents (6) in; the CDN does not block the User-Agent of OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Perplexity-User (12).

If a bot can't read the page, nothing else matters to it.

2. Content

150+ words in the delivered HTML (10), a visible answer with a main heading and paragraph (6), authorship (5), and date (5).

Authorship and date are what event sites and landing pages lack most.

3. Entity

Organization or Person with @id in the home page JSON-LD (8), sameAs for the official profiles (4), and a link to About or Contact (2).

This is the "who publishes" that course 2 teaches you to build.

4. llms.txt and WebMCP

llms.txt weighs 2: in the Ahrefs study (137 thousand domains, 06/15/2026), 97% of the files received no requests at all.

WebMCP weighs 8 in total: a tool registered with document.modelContext.registerTool() is worth 5. The specification is a W3C community draft (10/16/2026); the old API name earns partial credit, with a warning.

2026 yardstickWebMCP is preparation for agents, not a ranking factor. llms.txt is cheap and optional, not a citation lever.

03 · Get the score and change a weight

Run it from the ferramentas/agente-ready folder.

Terminal · your site's score
cd ferramentas/agente-ready
python3 agente-ready.py https://seusite.com --saida nota/seusite
Terminal · your weights
cp pesos.json meus-pesos.json
# open meus-pesos.json and change the "peso" of one criterion (e.g.: webmcp_api from 5 to 0)
python3 agente-ready.py https://seusite.com --pesos meus-pesos.json --saida nota/seusite-meus-pesos
Why I changed the weight (template)
# Weights for <seusite.com> — <dd/mm/aaaa>
critério: <id do critério>
peso original: <n>  →  meu peso: <n>
motivo: <e.g.: my audience doesn't use browser agents; WebMCP is a draft with no stable browser>
nota com pesos.json: <n>  ·  nota com meus pesos: <n>
Example from the repository
www.inema.club: nota 93/100 (5 páginas, 22 requisições).
  [falha, peso 2] Link para Sobre ou Contato na home: sem link /sobre, /about ou /contato

eventos.inema.pro: nota 69/100 (5 páginas, 15 requisições).
  [falha, peso 8] Organization ou Person com @id no JSON-LD da home: 0 entidade(s); com @id: 0
  [falha, peso 5] Autoria identificada: 0 de 5 com autor (meta, rel=author ou JSON-LD)
  [falha, peso 5] Data de publicação ou atualização: 0 de 5 com data (JSON-LD, meta article ou <time>)
  [falha, peso 5] Ferramentas WebMCP registradas com document.modelContext.registerTool(): nenhum registerTool() em 2 script(s) de mesma origem + inline

The same eventos.inema.pro with webmcp_api from 5 to 0 in meus-pesos.json:

eventos.inema.pro: nota 73/100 (5 páginas, 15 requisições).
  [falha, peso 8] Organization ou Person com @id no JSON-LD da home: 0 entidade(s); com @id: 0
  [falha, peso 5] Autoria identificada: 0 de 5 com autor (meta, rel=author ou JSON-LD)
  [falha, peso 5] Data de publicação ou atualização: 0 de 5 com data (JSON-LD, meta article ou <time>)
  [falha, peso 4] sameAs ligando a entidade aos perfis oficiais: sem sameAs na entidade

The math: 66 points earned out of 95 applicable gives 69; out of 90, it gives 73. The list of what to fix first did not change order at the top.

  • Treating the score as a ranking: the weights are an editorial decision by INEMA, with a source for the why, not for the number.
  • Changing a weight to make the score go up: change it when you have a reason and write it down; the criterion stays in the report.
  • Expecting to see a WebMCP tool from another origin: detection is static; a script from another domain, loaded later or in an iframe does not show up.

04 · Practice: the score and your weight

In lesson 7 the score joins the monthly routine.

E1 · The score

15 min

Run agente-ready on your site and write down the score and the 3 failing criteria with the highest weight.

Done when: you have the score, the 3 criteria with the weight of each, and the evidence the report shows.

E2 · Disagree with a reason

15 min

Copy pesos.json, change one weight with a written reason, and run again with --pesos.

Done when: the "why I changed the weight" template is filled in with both scores, and they are different.

06 · Takeaways

Open mathWeights, reason and source in pesos.json; whatever doesn't apply is left out of the denominator.
What weighs mostBot access (30) and extractable content (26) come before everything else.
2026 yardstickllms.txt is optional (weight 2); WebMCP is preparation, not ranking.
