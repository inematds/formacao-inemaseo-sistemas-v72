Lesson 4 · 17 min

gsc-historico: Search Console forever

Search Console keeps 16 months. Two years from now, the week your site started being found by people who don't know your brand will be gone. gsc-historico imports the weekly CSV into a local database that never deletes anything and builds the report that matters: branded, non-branded and anonymized, what went up, what went down, and the content backlog.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Where the CSV comes from and what "anonymized" means
  2. 2Import without duplicating and generate the report
  3. 3Read the weekly series and the up/down
  4. 4Content backlog and pages with no clicks: the tool's rules, not Google's

Goal: import the weekly CSVs into a database that doesn't delete, and read branded × non-branded × anonymized, the content backlog and the pages with no clicks.

02 · Read what the report shows

Click each list.

1. Series

Branded is people who already know you. Non-branded is new people arriving through the topic. Anonymized is what Google hides because it is rare: total minus branded minus non-branded.

Track non-branded impressions and clicks, week by week.

Real INEMA caseinema.club, from 09/16–22 to 10/05–11: non-branded impressions went from 8 to 157 and non-branded queries from 3 to 51, with 0 non-branded clicks. The first 2 non-branded clicks came on inema.pro (2/293), driven by "curso jev".

2. Up and down

Adds up the impressions and clicks of each query, current week against the previous one. The position shown is the best among the pages.

A query that drops several weeks in a row is a sign to look at its page.

3. Content backlog

A new query (never seen in the database) with no owner page goes into the content backlog: write a page made for it.

Owner page is a rule of this tool: any page on Google's 1st page, or with a word from the query in the URL.

You decideThe rule compares words with the URL, not with the content. A Spanish page may already show up for the query, off the 1st page, and the query still comes out as backlog. The paginas_hoje column shows who appears today.

4. No clicks

A page with 5+ non-branded impressions in the week and 0 clicks: rewrite the title and description with a specific promise.

It may have branded clicks; the list looks only at non-branded searches.

03 · Import and generate the report

Run it from the ferramentas/gsc-historico folder. The example uses the real inemaSEO CSVs that come in the repository.

Terminal · import (folder or files)
cd ferramentas/gsc-historico
python3 gsc_historico.py importar ../../exemplos/gsc-historico/entrada --db dados/gsc.sqlite
Terminal · report for one site
python3 gsc_historico.py relatorio --db dados/gsc.sqlite --site inema.club --saida saida/inema.club
# another comparison: --atual YYYY-MM-DD --anterior YYYY-MM-DD
Terminal · every week, with your CSVs
# after gsc_semanal.py (inemaseo-metodo), point to its folder
python3 gsc_historico.py importar ~/path/to/gsc-folder --db dados/gsc.sqlite
python3 gsc_historico.py relatorio --db dados/gsc.sqlite --site <yoursite.com> --saida saida/<yoursite.com>
Template · API mode (uses the Google API; not run in this course)
# runs gsc_semanal.py with your credential (gcloud) and imports; not tested live by the author
python3 gsc_historico.py puxar --script ~/projetos/inemaseo-metodo/tools/gsc_semanal.py --pasta dados/gsc-csv

The Spanish page already appears for these queries; the owner page rule does not recognize it because the words are not in the URL. Here the decision is to improve the page that exists, not to write another one.

  • Adding up overlapping weeks: 09/27–10/03 and 09/28–10/04 share days; the database keeps both and the report warns you.
  • Reading the content backlog as an order: it is a heuristic; check the column for the page that appears today.
  • Putting the database in git: it lives in dados/, which the .gitignore already excludes.

20 min
